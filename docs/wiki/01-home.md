# 1. Fysik

Fysik replaces Valheim's vanilla support, where a piece only cares about how far it is from the
ground, with a force calculation in which the **shape** of a build matters. Arches carry their load in
compression, braces and triangles carry it along their length, cantilevers bend, and everything
weighs: furniture, full chests, players walking across, a loaded cart rolling over a bridge and the
snow piling up on roofs in the Deep North. An overloaded piece cracks before it falls, so there is
time to shore it up.

> **Back up your world before installing.** Fysik is on from the start: buildings that do not respect
> its physics crack and fall, including bases built before it was installed. Starting a new world is
> recommended.

## Pages

- **Getting started**: installing, the three modes, old bases and your first builds.
- **Reading the forces**: the colors, the lines next to the crosshair, the X-ray, the placement
  preview and how cracking works.
- **What weighs**: pieces, furniture, piles, chests, players, carts and snow, with typical weights.
- **Building guide**: balconies, arches, trusses, stone masonry, round towers and stone bridges,
  snow on roofs and carts on bridges, with worked examples.
- **Server admins**: multiplayer, versions, world buildings, big bases and the settings worth knowing.
- **FAQ**: common questions, and how to send a build that behaves strangely.
- **How it works**: the calculation behind it, for the curious.
